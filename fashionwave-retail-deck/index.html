SLIDE 02

Business Context, Objectives & Analytical Assumptions

5 Core Management Questions

Diagnosing margin leakage, discount elasticity, store assortment divides, and weekday-vs-weekend traffic across 50 Indian urban stores.

1. Category Profitability

Identify which categories (Clothing, Shoes, Accessories) drive volume versus profit leverage and where margin is structurally vulnerable.

2. Discounting ROI & Elasticity

Test whether 16–30% promotional markdowns expand basket unit volume or merely cannibalize full-price operating profit.

3. Weekend vs Weekday Myth

Verify if Saturday–Sunday footfall warrants elevated weekend staffing rotas compared to Friday and Monday commute peaks.

4 & 5. Store Benchmarking & Channel Monetization

  • Store Network Chasm: Contrast top-quartile "Strong Performers" against bottom-tier stores using median Revenue & Profit crosshairs.
  • Customer & Tender Capture: Quantify the 10.4% Unknown CustomerType CRM gap and compare Digital UPI vs Cash vs Card basket economics.

Analytical Scope & Integrity Guardrails

  • Orders vs Unique Customers: No persistent customer ID exists in the dataset; frequency metrics strictly reflect Transaction Order Counts (no fabricated LTV/RFM).
  • Zero Hardcoded Metrics: All KPIs, ratios, regressions, and insights recalculate live in JavaScript from the filtered dataset.
  • Unknown Retention Governance: Retaining Unknown POS tags preserves 10.64% of net revenue and exposes checkout compliance gaps.

SLIDE 10

Strategic Recommendations, Execution Roadmap & Risks

Prioritized Quick Wins (0–30 Days) and Structural Initiatives (1–6 Months) targeting ₹3.3L+ annualized profit expansion.

⚡ Quick Wins (0–30 Days)

High Feasibility
  • Cap Unconditioned Discounts at 15%: Require 3+ items or ₹3,500 cart for >10% off. Impact: ₹85K–₹1.00L recovery | Risk: Shopper pushback (mitigate via bundle signage).
  • Level-Load Friday & Monday Staffing: Shift weekend-heavy rotas to Friday (#1) & Monday (#2) peaks. Impact: Higher conversion & labor ROI.
  • POS Mandatory Phone Capture & UPI QR: Close 10.4% Unknown CRM gap and shift Cash to UPI (+₹124 AOV).

🚀 Long-Term Initiatives (1–6 Months)

Structural Scale
  • Footwear Assortment Rebalancing: Enforce minimum Shoe SKU depth in all 23 "Needs Investigation" stores (fixing Store_25's 7.1x deficit). Impact: ₹1.8L–₹2.5L revenue | Risk: Working capital tie-up.
  • Checkout Accessory Attach Towers: Pair 35.25%-margin Accessories with Clothing/Shoes. Impact: ₹70K+ profit.
  • Pre-Festive Sep 15 Inventory Lock: Prevent stockouts ahead of October Diwali surge.
