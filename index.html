Case Study 1 · FashionWave Retail Store Sales Analysis (India)

Solving Weekend Sales Stagnation & Margin Leakage Across 50 Stores

FashionWave operates 50 retail stores across India. Weekend sales remain stagnant while weekday revenue holds steady. Management commissioned a 2-year empirical audit to lift weekend revenue by +15%, fix product-level profitability, and establish data-driven discounting guardrails.

3 Core Objectives

  • +15% Weekend Growth: Increase weekend sales next quarter via targeted promos & assortment.
  • Product Profitability: Identify underperforming SKUs/categories dragging margin.
  • Smart Discounting: Replace blanket markdowns with ROI-positive discount rules.

Tracked Business KPIs

  • Total Sales: Net revenue per store per week & network total.
  • AOV & Margin %: Revenue per transaction & (Profit / Net Revenue).
  • Units Sold & Discount Impact: Category volume & margin delta with vs without discount.
  • Weekend / Weekday Ratio: Weekend vs Weekday revenue velocity.

Scope & Constraints

  • 2-Year Window: Nov 2024 – Nov 2026 (1,500 transactions, 50 stores).
  • 30% Policy Cap: Discounts above 30% are restricted by corporate policy.
  • Seasonality & Data Hygiene: Festival effects apply; raw blanks in Discount & CustomerType require cleaning.
